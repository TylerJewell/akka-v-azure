Feb 13, 2025 · 1 min read

About this webinar

Event sourcing is transforming how we design, build, and maintain modern applications. By capturing changes to application state as a series of immutable events, this architectural pattern simplifies some of the toughest distributed problems.

In this webinar, Kevin Hoffman—author of Real-World Event Sourcing and Director of Product Management at Akka—shares key insights from his book, demonstrating event sourcing with examples in Akka. Developers, architects, and technical leaders will walk away with the knowledge and tools needed to implement event sourcing efficiently in their systems.
