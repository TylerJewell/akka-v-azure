About this podcast

Tyler Jewell, CEO of Akka and creator of the Developer-Led Landscape, shares how agentic AI is reshaping system architecture. He discusses real-world engineering challenges, scaling to 2M TPS, balancing cost vs. quality, and the role of actor-based concurrency in managing complexity. Tyler also highlights how Akka contributes to agentic AI and what’s next for building resilient, scalable AI systems.
