Session Abstract

Most enterprises are stuck between AI prototypes and production. The gaps: reliability, risk, and repeatability. Tyler Jewell, CEO of Akka, shares how industry leaders can embed policy enforcement, compliance, and failure containment directly in the agentic runtime to scale confidently across teams. Join us to learn why bolt-on governance fails and how embedding compliance in the agentic runtime can close the pilot-to-production gap, scale agentic AI across teams, and prepare for evolving AI compliance requirements.
