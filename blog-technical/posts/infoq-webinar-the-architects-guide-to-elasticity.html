About this webinar

This webinar pushes architects and senior developers to rethink elasticity. It’s crucial to consider all external factors that affect elasticity, such as user traffic, workload variations, version and schema changes, network issues, and hardware failures.

Jonas Bonér, a leading expert in distributed systems, explores both the technical and business benefits of elasticity, as well as the challenges in achieving it. He discusses the pros and cons of common approaches like database replication and the different levels of elasticity. Jonas also delves into the science behind elasticity and the practical implications of various technology and architecture choices.
