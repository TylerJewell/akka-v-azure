Creating Certainty in the Age of Agentic AI

Team Akka
Jul 17, 2025 · 1 min read

About this webinar

Akka CEO Tyler Jewell and Principal Solutions Architect Alan Klikić explore what it takes to move from agent prototypes to fully operational agentic AI systems—faster and with greater enterprise confidence.

Learn how leading enterprises are building with confidence by using the right architecture, tools, and patterns to meet demanding SLAs and eliminate the risks of uncertainty. The Akka team also shares new innovations designed to help enterprises deliver agentic systems 3x faster, with 1/3 the compute, and lower token consumption.
