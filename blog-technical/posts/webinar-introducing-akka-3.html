About this webinar

In this webinar, Akka leaders Jonas Bonér (Founder and CTO), Tyler Jewell (CEO), and Dan Rosanova (Senior VP of Product Management) share and demonstrate game-changing innovation for distributed applications.

The next generation of Akka offers:

  • A new SDK to make development simple
  • Serverless and BYOC operating environments
  • First app runtime that has Multi-Master Replication
  • First PaaS that migrates apps across hyperscalers
  • A resilience guarantee for your apps
