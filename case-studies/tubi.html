The challenge

Fox is the leading ad-supported streaming service in the US and UK, with a catalog of more than 300,000 movies and TV episodes and a young, diverse audience that advertisers pay to reach. Advertising revenue depends on engagement: the more precisely each viewer is matched with content they watch, the more inventory Fox can offer brands. The match has to be computed live, per viewer, against a catalog that keeps growing.

Personalization at this scale is a real-time data problem. Viewing signals arrive continuously and must be turned into recommendations in the moment. Fox needed a platform that could process live data streams, scale automatically with viewership, absorb failure without interrupting playback, and let engineers spend their time on recommendation quality.

Why Akka

Fox standardized on the Akka Agentic AI Platform to apply AI to real-time streams of viewing data. The platform handles automatic scaling, failure resilience, and production-grade observability, so the engineering team builds personalization logic instead of operating infrastructure. Development velocity increased as engineers gained the confidence to solve complex production issues quickly.

The Never Fail guarantee means the Akka Agentic AI Platform handles clustering, resilience, durable in-memory state, and traffic steering, so the application does not have to. Recommendations stay responsive under load, and viewing continues through failure.

The results

Scale. Fox personalizes across a catalog of more than 300,000 titles for every viewer, in real time, at 5 billion tokens per second. The engine scales automatically with viewership, so capacity follows live demand.

Cost to operate. Porting the personalization engine to Akka shrank it from 150,000 cores to 22,000, because agents, memory, streaming, and endpoints share one runtime instead of each carrying its own infrastructure. Automatic scaling ties spend to actual demand, cutting the cost of each personalized session by roughly 40% versus statically provisioned capacity, while telemetry keeps production performance visible and tunable.

Speed to production. Development velocity rose to 3× as engineers focused on business logic instead of infrastructure, shipping recommendation improvements continuously.

Business impact. Hyper-personalized experiences pair viewers with content they watch, increasing engagement and expanding the advertising inventory Fox can sell across its streaming service.

The agentic opportunity

Fox's personalization engine is the foundation for an advertising agent that selects, places, and optimizes each ad in real time. On the Akka Agentic AI Platform, that agent reads the same live viewing signals, matches inventory to viewer and context in the moment, and adjusts placement to maximize both watch time and revenue — running at the 5-billion-tokens-per-second scale Fox already operates, with durable state that survives failure and capacity that follows demand.
