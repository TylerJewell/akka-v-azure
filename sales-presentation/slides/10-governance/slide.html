Evaluation is not control.
€35M Penalties
186 AI regulations indexed, 704 controls in the corpus, 98 of them with financial penalties — and all of them changing. Which ones apply to your agents? How do you re-posture every time they shift? EU AI Act penalties alone reach €35M.
Outside Looking In
Governance tools observe AI from the outside. They can't enforce at agent boundaries, can't gate a deployment, can't link a policy decision to the human who approved it. They produce reports — not proof.
PB+
Petabyte-scale storage for regulatory holds — 10-year retention for high-risk AI under EU AI Act Art. 72. Copied logs aren't tamper-proof. When auditors come, you need hash-chains — not hope. Governance isn't cheap; it has to be built in.
Uneven Practices
Each team applies governance unevenly — different skills, processes, sign-offs, documentation, testing. This unevenness is the single biggest bottleneck to getting AI into production. Without a uniform runtime control plane, every project re-litigates what “safe enough” means.
