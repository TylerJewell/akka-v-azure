NTT DATA is a global IT services and consulting leader, headquartered in Tokyo and serving clients in more than 70 countries. Its Smart AI Agent Ecosystem is an enterprise-grade platform for building, adopting, managing, and scaling AI agents — with industry-specific agents across healthcare, automotive, and financial services, embedded governance and Responsible AI, and a marketplace of agents and models. Where Akka fits

An enterprise agentic ecosystem needs a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in rather than bolted on.
