About Carahsoft

Carahsoft is the Master Government Aggregator for the U.S. public sector, headquartered in Reston, Virginia. It makes leading technology available to federal, defense, intelligence, state, local, education, and healthcare agencies through more than 220 contract vehicles — including GSA MAS, NASA SEWP V, NASPO ValuePoint, and OMNIA Partners — and a network of thousands of reseller partners.

Carahsoft runs a dedicated Artificial Intelligence Solutions for Government portfolio spanning AI infrastructure, generative and agentic AI, autonomous systems, and AI governance — and has brought agentic-AI vendors to the public sector as Master Aggregator, making emerging AI available to agencies through the vehicles they already use.

Where Akka fits

Government agentic AI has to run on a platform that is resilient, secure, and governed. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and policy controls built into the runtime, benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second. Carahsoft makes it procurable through the contract vehicles agencies already hold; agencies run it in production.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: for mission-critical government workloads, resilience and governance are handled by the runtime, not left to the application.
