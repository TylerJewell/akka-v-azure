About BAE Systems

BAE Systems is Europe's largest defense, security, and aerospace company and a prime contractor to the U.S. Department of Defense, with around 100,000 employees. It is embedding agentic AI directly into operational platforms and mission systems — combat vehicles, deterrence programs, and future platforms — to reshape command and control and create a human-machine advantage at the edge.

BAE frames agentic AI as mission-outcome oriented: systems that support operators in high-stakes, contested environments by processing information and adapting during missions, translating sensing into real-time, coordinated effects for faster, better-informed decisions.

Where Akka fits

Mission-critical agentic AI cannot fail, cannot lose state, and cannot go ungoverned. The Akka Agentic AI Platform is built for exactly these requirements — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and policy controls built into the runtime, benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

For agentic AI operating at the edge of a mission, Never Fail and Self-Governing are not features; they are the requirement. Resilience, durable state, and governance are handled by the runtime, so mission systems stay reliable and accountable under the hardest conditions.
