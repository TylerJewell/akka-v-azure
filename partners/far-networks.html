About Far Networks

Far Networks is an Italian system integrator founded in 2006, headquartered near Milan. It delivers enterprise infrastructure, software development, cloud, and digital-workplace solutions to more than 500 organizations, including well-known Italian and international brands. Its focus is integrating and delivering the technology that runs an enterprise's core operations.

As enterprises adopt agentic AI, Far Networks brings the delivery and integration experience to put it into production.

Where Akka fits

Putting agentic AI into production takes a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load — and a partner to integrate and deliver it. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides the runtime; Far Networks brings the delivery. As Far Networks integrates and deploys, Akka is the platform its customers run those systems on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime, so the AI Far Networks delivers stays reliable and governed.
