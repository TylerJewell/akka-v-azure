## About VirtusLab

VirtusLab is a software consulting and engineering firm founded in 2010, with a 400-plus EU-based team. It is a core contributor to and maintainer of the Scala toolchain — Metals, scala-cli, scalafmt, and more — across 193 open-source repositories, and delivers data engineering, cloud-native, frontend, and high-performing reactive backend systems.

VirtusLab's AI work is engineering-centric: it created Metals MCP, a bridge exposing Scala compiler internals to LLM agents, and publishes an ongoing guide to agentic programming on the JVM. Its focus is connecting agents to real enterprise JVM systems rather than generic AI strategy.

## Where Akka fits

Agentic AI built for enterprise JVM systems needs a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in rather than bolted on. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that. As VirtusLab engineers agents into JVM systems, Akka is the platform its customers run them on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime — the reactive, JVM-grade reliability VirtusLab engineers for.
