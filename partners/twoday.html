About Twoday

Twoday is a Nordic IT services group with 2,300 experts across Denmark, Finland, Norway, Sweden, and Lithuania. Its Applied AI practice spans AI strategy and governance, agent and automation development, enterprise AI platforms, and AI scaling. Its Agentic Orchestration Studio is an agent-agnostic layer that coordinates agents, people, and systems inside governed processes — turning AI activity into measurable financial performance with control and accountability.

Twoday builds around governance and accountability, and holds top-tier partnerships across the major cloud and data platforms in the Nordics.

Where Akka fits

Orchestrating agents inside governed processes still needs a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in rather than bolted on. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that. As Twoday builds and orchestrates agents, Akka is the platform their customers run those agents on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime — the same accountability Twoday designs into every process, enforced where the agents actually run.
