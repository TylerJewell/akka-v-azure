About SHI International

SHI International is a value-added reseller and IT solutions provider headquartered in Somerset, New Jersey — the largest minority- and woman-owned business enterprise in the United States, with roughly 6,000 employees and more than 15,000 customer organizations, and over $16 billion in sales. It serves enterprise, federal, defense, state, local, education, and academic markets through contract vehicles including GSA MAS, NASA SEWP V, and the NASPO ValuePoint software reseller cooperative.

SHI operates an AI & Cyber Labs facility that helps customers move AI use cases from ideation to production, and has expanded its AI capabilities through investments and partnerships across AI engineering and infrastructure.

Where Akka fits

Enterprise and government agentic AI has to run on a platform that is resilient, secure, and governed. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and policy controls built into the runtime, benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second. SHI makes it available across its markets; customers run it in production.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience and governance are handled by the runtime, so agentic AI stays reliable across enterprise and public-sector workloads.
