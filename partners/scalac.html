About Scalac

Scalac is a software and AI consultancy with more than a decade of production-grade Scala and JVM engineering, headquartered in San Francisco with an R&D center in Gdańsk. It has repositioned around agentic AI with three productized offerings: production-ready multi-agent systems where agents plan, reason, and act; private, self-hosted LLMs deployed inside a client's own VPC; and agentic RAG combining vector search, knowledge graphs, and autonomous reasoning. Scalac emphasizes security and control — sovereign AI, isolated in the client's infrastructure — across fintech, SaaS, and healthtech.

Scalac is an Official Akka Tech Partner, and brings its reactive-systems heritage — Kafka, Spark, and the Akka ecosystem — to building intelligent, autonomous systems.

Where Akka fits

Multi-agent systems that plan, reason, and act need a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in rather than bolted on. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that. As Scalac builds agentic systems, Akka is the platform its customers run them on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime — matching the security and sovereignty Scalac engineers into every deployment.
