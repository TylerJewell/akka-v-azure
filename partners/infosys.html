About Infosys

Infosys Topaz is Infosys's AI-first set of services, solutions, and platforms. Infosys was among the first global IT services providers to treat AI agents not as add-ons but as primary actors in service delivery. Through Agentic Foundry and Topaz Fabric, Infosys builds reliable, production-grade AI agents from reusable, composable components, and has deployed more than 200 enterprise agents across healthcare, finance, retail, telecom, manufacturing, and agriculture.

Infosys builds this way deliberately: agentic AI adopted responsibly and ethically, with no technology lock-in, so enterprises future-proof their AI investments.

Where Akka fits

Production-grade agentic AI needs a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in rather than bolted on. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that. As Infosys builds and deploys agents across industries, Akka is the platform their customers run those agents on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime, so the agents Infosys builds stay reliable and governed as they expand across the enterprise.
