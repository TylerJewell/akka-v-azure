About Improving

Improving is an IT consulting and software development firm founded in 2007, headquartered in Dallas, with offices across the Americas and India and 2,500+ technologists. It modernizes core systems, unlocks data, and embeds intelligence for enterprises — platform engineering, secure integration, real-time data and lakehouse modernization — alongside expert-led training in AI, agile, and leadership.

Improving has been an Akka Platform Partner since 2016, delivering architecture assessments, prototype development, managed services, and Reactive Architecture training. Its Akka work includes the reservation-system modernization it delivered for Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings.

Where Akka fits

Modernizing a core system — and running agentic AI on top of it — takes a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that. As Improving modernizes and builds, Akka is the platform its customers run those systems on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime, so modernized systems and the agents built on them stay reliable and governed.
