About AVOWS

AVOWS is a technology consultancy founded in 2008, serving enterprises across Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, India, and the United States. It delivers a broad digital-transformation portfolio — software engineering, cloud, data analytics, automation, cybersecurity, and agentic AI — connecting enterprises with specialist technology talent.

With more than 1,000 consultants and 200+ clients, AVOWS brings the regional delivery reach to put agentic AI into production across Southeast Asia.

Where Akka fits

Putting agentic AI into production takes a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in — and a partner to deliver it. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides the runtime; AVOWS brings the delivery. As AVOWS builds and deploys, Akka is the platform its customers run those systems on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime, so the AI AVOWS delivers stays reliable and governed as it scales.
