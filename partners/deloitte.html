The engagement

Manulife set out to move agentic AI beyond experimentation into mission-critical production across a global insurance business. That required a platform to build and run a high volume of AI agents against critical workflows, with security, reliability, and Responsible AI governance built into the foundation rather than added later.

Deloitte's Applied AI practice in Canada leads enterprises through exactly this transition: taking agentic AI past the proof-of-concept phase and scaling it to mission-critical deployments. Deloitte and Akka partnered to take Manulife through it, pairing that delivery practice with the runtime the platform stands on.

Why Deloitte and Akka

Deloitte is the Applied AI expert — strategy, integration, and Responsible AI practice. Akka is the Agentic AI Platform those systems run on: a durable, highly available runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load. Together they deliver production AI systems and lead organizational AI transformation. For Manulife, that is a secure, governed foundation for high-volume, business-critical AI.

This is the Self-Governing platform: safeguards, controls, and Responsible AI practices are embedded in the runtime, not bolted on, so governance holds as AI expands across critical workflows.

The results

Foundation. Deloitte and Akka delivered Manulife's enterprise agentic AI platform — a secure, integrated foundation for building and deploying AI agents across the organization, with governance and Responsible AI embedded.

Scale and efficiency. The platform builds and runs a high volume of trusted AI applications on the Akka Agentic AI Platform, benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second, at 3× development velocity on a third of the compute.

Governance. Strong safeguards and Responsible AI practices are embedded in the runtime as AI is adopted across critical workflows.

Delivery. Deloitte's Applied AI practice and the Akka Agentic AI Platform delivered production AI end to end, from strategy through operation, and lead Manulife's organizational AI transformation.

The joint agentic opportunity

Manulife's business — underwriting, claims, advisor and customer support — is where governed agentic AI now operates: agents that assess risk, adjudicate claims, and support advisors and customers in real time, each running under embedded policy controls and audit. On the Akka Agentic AI Platform, those agents run with durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and the scale a global insurer requires.

The platform Deloitte and Akka delivered at Manulife is the same platform any enterprise runs agentic AI on. Firms engage Deloitte to co-deliver it, or build directly on the Akka Agentic AI Platform. Both provide the production and governance guarantees Manulife runs on.
