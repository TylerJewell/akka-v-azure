About PwC

PwC is one of the world's largest professional services networks, operating in 149 countries. Through agent OS, PwC provides a platform to build, orchestrate, and govern AI agents across an enterprise — cross-vendor by design, working across the major clouds, model providers, and enterprise applications. PwC pairs the platform with AI strategy consulting to move clients beyond AI experimentation into enterprise-wide adoption, orchestrating whole workflows rather than automating single tasks.

PwC has committed nearly $1.5 billion to next-generation AI capabilities. In its 2025 AI Agent Survey, 79% of executives reported AI agents already in use and 88% planned to increase AI budgets.

Where Akka fits

Orchestrating agents across an enterprise is one problem; running them reliably in production is another. Agentic AI needs a runtime that holds state, recovers from failure, and scales under load, with governance built in rather than bolted on. The Akka Agentic AI Platform provides exactly that. As PwC builds and orchestrates agents for its clients, Akka is the platform those clients run them on in production — durable state that survives failure, sub-second recovery, and scale benchmarked to 10 million transactions per second.

This is the Never Fail and Self-Governing platform: resilience, durable state, and policy controls are handled by the runtime, so the agents PwC orchestrates stay reliable and governed across the enterprise.
