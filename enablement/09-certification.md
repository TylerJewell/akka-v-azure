# Certification

Each level gates the next. A learner cannot run a scenario before passing routing, and cannot
take a customer meeting alone before Field certification.

---

## Level 1 — Foundation certified

**Gate for:** the solution modules.

| Assessment | Standard |
|---|---|
| The 90-second platform statement | Delivered live from memory in 90 seconds or under, every number correct, four proofs attached to the right solutions |
| The 20-question fact check | 18 correct. Questions in `00-foundation.md` §10 |
| Disqualifiers | Names four of the five without prompting |

A learner who states a number the source files do not carry fails regardless of the other scores. The whole program depends on that discipline.

## Level 2 — Routing certified

**Gate for:** the scenario library and any customer meeting with a colleague present.

| Assessment | Standard |
|---|---|
| Four solution discovery calls | The exercise at the end of each solution module, 20 minutes each, passed individually |
| The routing drill | 13 of 15 correct in under 30 seconds each. `05-routing.md` §8 |
| The mandate-owner meeting | The 25-minute first meeting in `06-mandate-owners.md` §9 |

The routing drill is retaken in full on failure. Partial retakes let a learner memorise the items they missed instead of learning the model.

## Level 3 — Field certified

**Gate for:** running a customer meeting alone, and for carrying a forecast.

| Assessment | Standard |
|---|---|
| Competitive role plays | All three in `07-competitive.md` §10. A concession before every contrast, one sourced claim per role play, no unsupported absolute |
| Two scenarios | Different entry solutions, 8 of 10 on the standard sheet, plus every scenario-specific criterion met |
| Scenario 1, 3, or 9 | One of these three must be among the two. They carry the routing failures that cost the most deals |

Any scenario-specific criterion marked "and the scenario fails" is disqualifying regardless of the point total. The disqualifying criteria are overclaiming on LangGraph durability, leaving the technical owner
an opponent, overclaiming the Verify coverage line, positioning against a partner, failing to
name a funder, accepting a portfolio proof of concept, and positioning centralisation in front
of the engineering owner.

## Level 4 — Track certified

**Gate for:** owning an account in that role.

| Role | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Account executive | The account plan exercise on a live opportunity, defended in ten minutes including the tier choice |
| Forward-deployed engineer | A scenario carried to signed engagement, plus the Frame deliverable with a four-to-six-week milestone plan |
| Field CTO and solution architect | Whiteboards 1, 3, and 4 delivered in twelve minutes to a hostile audience, plus a written technical win plan |
| Partner | Scenario 8 as learner, scenario 5 as facilitator |

## The standard scenario sheet

Scored out of ten. Eight to pass.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routed on the stated pain | Led with the wrong solution | Right solution, arrived at late | Right solution from the first exchange |
| Quantified the cost in the customer's numbers | No number | Akka's number | The customer's own number |
| Conceded accurately before contrasting | Attacked a competitor | Generic concession | Named what the incumbent is genuinely good at |
| Named a second cost owned by someone absent | None named | Named a cost, no person | Named the cost and the person |
| Secured a dated next step | None | Vague follow-up | A date, a name, and a deliverable |

## Recertification

| Trigger | What is retaken |
|---|---|
| A change to the four-cost table or to any headline figure | The fact check |
| A new competitor category or a material battlecard revision | The competitive role plays |
| Six months without a closed opportunity in a solution | That solution's discovery call |
| Annually | The routing drill |

Every number in this program traces to the Akka positioning reference. When it changes, the
field carries stale figures until the fact check is retaken, which makes it the
recertification that cannot be deferred.

## Records

Track per person: level attained, date, assessor, scenarios run and scores, and the date each
recertification falls due. Partners are tracked by firm and by individual, because partner
staff turn over faster than the recertification cadence assumes.
