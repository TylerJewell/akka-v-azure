# Akka Field Enablement Program

For account executives, forward-deployed engineers, field CTOs, and delivery partners.

## What this program trains

Akka sells one offering, the Akka Agentic AI Platform. Four solutions run on it, and each
removes a different cost from a different budget. Which solution leads a conversation depends
on which cost the person in the room pays.

The skill this program builds is routing: hear what a person owns, lead with the solution
that removes their cost, and name a second cost owned by someone who is not in the room. That
second cost is the follow-up meeting, and it belongs to a different budget holder every time.
A rep who routes well leaves every first meeting multi-threaded.

Product knowledge supports that skill without being the goal.

## The cost each solution removes

Learn this table before anything else. Everything after it is detail.

| Solution | Cost removed | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| **Akka SDK** | Infrastructure | Fox shrank its AI personalization engine from 150,000 cores to 22,000 |
| **Akka Specify** | Rework | Dojo put AI-based merchant onboarding into production in weeks, built by college graduates |
| **Akka Optimize** | Token spend | Swiggy cut prediction latency from 144ms to 71ms while reducing token consumption 22% |
| **Akka Verify** | Governance effort | Manulife rolled Akka out to 2,000 developers in 6 countries under central risk control |

## What each module covers

**Foundation.** What Akka is, one offering and four solutions, why execution efficiency
decides the market, the guarantees no other vendor offers, what Akka is not, and the
disqualifiers. The module also covers why one runtime with one correct form per problem lets any team, and any AI coding assistant, produce systems that reach production.

**The four solution modules.** For each solution: the cost, who pays it, the customer
challenge, the capabilities and core features, the mechanism, the differentiators against
each competitor category, the named proof, the use cases and quick wins, the discovery
questions, the objections, and what to send after the call.

**Routing.** The model that decides which solution leads, the persona map, trigger events,
industry defaults, and the five routing failures.

**Mandate owners.** AI Transformation Officers and anyone who owns an AI outcome across the organisation instead of one of the four costs. The routing model does not work on them, and
this module covers what does.

**Competitive.** Frameworks, hyperscalers, SaaS agents, observability tools, integrators, and GRC platforms, what each is genuinely good at, what the customer
inherits from each, the wedge, and the question that proves it. Plus what competitors say
about Akka and how to answer.

**Role tracks.** Qualification, service tiers, and the business case for account executives.
The delivery methodology and production-slice scoping for forward-deployed engineers. The
whiteboards, the demo sequence, and the technical win plan for field CTOs. Sourcing,
registration, and co-delivery for partners.

**Scenarios.** Ten account simulations, each running across three to five meetings with
different people. Each brief names the account and the cast. The meetings run live in
session.

| Module | Self-paced | Live |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 2 hours | 90 minutes |
| Each solution module | 90 minutes | 2 hours |
| Routing | 1 hour | 2 hours |
| Mandate owners | 1 hour | 2 hours |
| Competitive | 2 hours | 2 hours of role play |
| Each scenario | — | 2 hours, in groups of three |
| Role track | 3 hours | Half a day |

Take the modules in order through Competitive. Scenarios and the role track run in either
order after that.

## What you will be asked to do

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Deliver the 90-second platform statement live from memory, and pass a 20-question fact check |
| Routing | Run a 20-minute discovery call for each solution, and name the entry solution and follow-up cost for 15 cold opens in under 30 seconds each |
| Competitive | Three head-to-head role plays, conceding accurately before every contrast |
| Field | Two scenarios run to a scored outcome |
| Role track | The exercise for your role |

A new account executive reaches routing certification in the second week and scenario
certification in the fourth. A field CTO carries the same foundation and spends the extra
time in the solution modules at mechanism depth.
