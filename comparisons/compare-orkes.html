Compare — Akka vs. Orkes

Orkes Conductor is an orchestration engine. Akka is a platform.

Orkes Conductor orchestrates your workflows and agents. But an agentic system is far more than an orchestration engine — it also needs agents, memory, streaming, APIs, and governance. With Orkes you source, integrate, and operate each of those yourself. Akka delivers them as one runtime, on the same durability principles, at higher guarantees.

Orkes vs. Akka

A durable-execution engine beside a full agentic platform.

Orkes — Orchestration engine

You build the rest and operate it

Orkes Conductor gives you a durable, event-driven engine for orchestrating long-running workflows and agents, descended from Netflix Conductor. Durable execution covers the workflow layer of an agentic system. In Conductor an agent is a workflow that calls an LLM to decide its next step; the durable memory, the streaming tier, the API layer, and the AI-governance stack are pieces you select, wire together, and operate around the orchestration core — and you own every failure across those seams.

Orkes Cloud runs that orchestration layer under an availability SLA of up to 99.99% on multi-region clusters. State lives in external datastores you provision and operate — Redis by default, Elasticsearch for indexing, pluggable Postgres, MySQL, or Cassandra — each under its own availability terms.

Akka — Platform

It comes built and operated

Akka gives you agents, durable state, streaming, APIs, and governance as one pre-integrated runtime — built on the same durable-execution principles that orchestration engines are known for, extended to the whole system. You describe the system you want, and the platform generates it, tests it, and runs it. Because everything runs in one runtime, a system that compiles is ready for production.

Akka keeps it running around the clock, with active-active replication and automatic failover, and stands behind a contractual 99.9999% availability SLA on the entire platform, backed by indemnities.

Governance & the EU AI Act

Bolt on, or enforce.

The difference is where governance runs: inside the runtime, or in a separate service alongside it. Orkes certifies the infrastructure and leaves AI governance to tools you add on top. With Akka, risk and compliance write the policies independently of the agents, and the runtime enforces them on every action — with no developer code to customize.

Orkes — Bolt-on

After the fact

Add it around the engine

Orkes holds SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA through isolated-cloud deployment — security compliance for the infrastructure — plus role-based access control, secret management, and audit logs. Temporal publishes no AI-governance capability: no real-time policy enforcement, no decision explainability, no human pause or override of a running agent as a platform primitive, no immutable interaction ledger, and no pre-deployment classification.

Any AI governance is a separate tool you add and operate. Log-based tools report after the fact and cannot gate a deployment or stop an action before it happens.

Akka — Enforce

Before the action

Block & escalate

With Akka, your risk and compliance teams write the policies themselves, in a separate versioned lifecycle that is independent of the agents — there is no developer code to customize. The runtime enforces every policy inline at the agent boundary, so human-in-the-loop approvals, human-on-the-loop halt switches, sanitizers, guardrails, evaluations, testing gates, and simulations all run before an action executes and block or escalate anything that does not pass.

Every call is traced automatically into petabyte-scale, tamper-evident storage, and that same trace data feeds continuous evaluations that keep running in production, right alongside your agents.

Choosing between them

Pick for what you are building.

Choose the engine when

Reach for Orkes when you need durable orchestration of long-running workflows, when the work is coordinating steps and tools inside a larger system, and when your team is ready to select, integrate, and operate the memory, streaming, API, and governance layers around the orchestration core. Conductor carries a real orchestration heritage from Netflix and runs in production at large enterprises.

Choose the platform when

Reach for Akka when you are building an agentic system — agents, memory, streaming, APIs, and governance — and you want them pre-integrated and operated under one SLA, with recovery and governance built in on the same durability principles.
