Compare — Akka vs. Google Gemini Enterprise

Gemini Enterprise is a cloud agent suite. Akka is a platform.

Gemini Enterprise gives you Google Cloud building blocks to assemble inside Google Cloud. But a production agentic system needs agents, memory, streaming, APIs, and governance unified under one SLA — and bound to no single cloud. With Gemini Enterprise you integrate separate Google Cloud services and stay on GCP for identity, models, and data. Akka delivers the whole system as one operated, governed platform that runs on any cloud, on-prem, or sovereign.

Gemini Enterprise vs. Akka

Assembled pieces, or one platform.

Gemini Enterprise — Cloud agent suite

You assemble it on Google Cloud

Gemini Enterprise is the name Google gave Vertex AI at Cloud Next 2026. Gemini Enterprise carries a code-first Agent Development Kit, a low-code Agent Studio, and templates in Agent Garden, plus a managed Agent Engine runtime and a Model Garden of models. Each is a separate Google Cloud service the customer wires together and operates; the platform unifies the console and the namespace; the runtime underneath stays a set of services.

Google Cloud publishes a 99.9% SLA for online inference, and the Agent Runtime itself is excluded from the SLA. Identity runs through Google Cloud IAM, data lives in your GCP project, and models are served through GCP.

Akka — Platform

It comes built, operated, and portable

Akka Platform gives you agents, durable memory, streaming, APIs, and governance as one pre-integrated runtime. You describe the system you want, and the platform generates it, tests it, and runs it — you do not integrate or operate the seams between an orchestration kit, a memory store, a streaming tier, and a governance stack.

Akka keeps it running around the clock with active-active replication and automatic failover, under a contractual 99.9999% availability SLA across the entire platform, backed by indemnities. Akka deploys on Akka cloud, any hyperscaler VPC, your own Kubernetes, on-prem, or a sovereign cloud — the specs are portable.

Governance & the EU AI Act

Bolt on, or enforce.

The difference is where governance runs: inside the runtime, or in a separate service alongside it. Gemini Enterprise secures the perimeter and the data with Google Cloud controls and leaves AI governance to tools you add on top. With Akka, risk and compliance write the policies independently of the agents, and the runtime enforces them on every action — with no developer code to customize.

Gemini Enterprise — Bolt-on

After the fact

Secure the perimeter

Gemini Enterprise provides infrastructure governance through Google Cloud IAM, VPC Service Controls, Customer-Managed Encryption Keys, Data Loss Prevention, Access Transparency, audit logging, and data residency. Google Cloud's controls secure the perimeter and the data.

The obligations of a named regulation are not enforced inline to the runtime: there is no real-time guardrail or judge layer woven into the runtime, no decision explainability, no human pause or override of a running agent as a platform primitive, no immutable interaction ledger, and no pre-deployment classification against a regulatory corpus.

Akka — Enforce

Before the action

Block & escalate

With Akka, your risk and compliance teams write the policies themselves, in a separate versioned lifecycle that is independent of the agents — there is no developer code to customize. The runtime enforces every policy inline at the agent boundary, so human-in-the-loop approvals, human-on-the-loop halt switches, sanitizers, guardrails, evaluations, testing gates, and simulations all run before an action executes and block or escalate anything that does not pass.

Every call is traced automatically into petabyte-scale, tamper-evident storage, and that same trace data feeds continuous evaluations that keep running in production, right alongside your agents. A system is classified against 190 regulations and 1,040 controls before it ships.

Choosing between them

Pick for where you are.

Choose the cloud agent suite when

Reach for Gemini Enterprise when your organization is standardized on Google Cloud and intends to stay there, when you want to build agents with Google's ADK, Agent Studio, and Model Garden inside the GCP console, and when your team is ready to integrate and operate the memory, streaming, API, and governance layers around the agent runtime as separate Google Cloud services.

Choose the platform when

Reach for Akka when you are building a full agentic system — agents, memory, streaming, APIs, and governance — and you want them pre-integrated and operated under one SLA, with inline governance built in, and the freedom to run on any cloud, on-prem, or a sovereign environment without rebinding to one vendor's identity, models, and data.
