What you pay for

You integrate numerous AWS services that are provided with Akka.

AWS uses meter-based pricing across high margin services to calculate your bill. Akka only uses AWS commodity infrastructure.

Included means covered by the fixed annual Akka fee.

N/A marks a service the AgentCore stack does not require. AgentCore Runtime is serverless, so it carries no cluster hours and no instance hours. Its memory and its state are managed services with their own meters. Its agent traces land in CloudWatch.

EKS, EC2, EC2 GPU capacity, RDS and S3 are the infrastructure Akka runs on. The customer buys them from AWS at published unit prices, and the Akka fee does not move with those meters. Akka writes interaction records, evidence and policy artifacts to S3.

Akka serves open-weight models on the customer's own GPU capacity, so token-metered inference is a choice. Routing to Bedrock stays available and bills per token on the requests sent there.
